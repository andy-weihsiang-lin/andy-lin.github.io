My wife and I have some old laptops that had been obsolete for more than 6 years. To keep them away from dust, we placed them deep in the storage cabinet, which made taking up space their only functionality.

We have recently identified our strong need for shared storage within our network for we have constantly looking for possibilities of file sharing and data management from time to time.

I had known that NAS is probably what can be of huge help. Yet, the recent demands of RAM and hard drives have led to dramatically high prices, and this significantly impeded my intention.

Fortunately, I happened to learn that spare laptops can be turned into NAS, so that’s why we have this installation today.

It is a Lenovo Thinkpad T470p with

  • Processor: Intel Core i7-7700HQ CPU @ 2.80GHz * 4
  • Graphic Card: NVIDIA Corporation GM 108M [GeForce 940MX]
  • Memory: 16 GB
  • Hard Drive: 2 TB SSD
  • Operating System: Linux Mint 22.3 - Cinnamon 64-bit

This lightweight economical solution works surprisingly well.
