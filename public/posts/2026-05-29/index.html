We installed our first NAS in our network on May 25. Since its installation, despite it being capable of humble 2 TB-storage, I cannot stop thinking of what we can do with the storage on server.

The first thing came to my mind is to run a self-host website.

I have always wanted to build a website without permissions from any third-party providers or some sort of subscription to make my MPV work. Yet, not until this point did I realize that I can actually locally run a website simply with some tools like Hugo.
