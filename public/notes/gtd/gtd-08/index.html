Date: 2026-06-01
Time: 21:57

Block out two hours early Friday afternoon to weekly review the system.

  • The events of the weekly are still fresh enough for you to be able to do a complete postmortem.
  • When uncovering actions that requires reaching colleagues, you still have time to do that before they leave for the weekend.
  • It is great to clear your psychic deck so you can go into the weekend ready for refreshment and recreation.

Targets are the following:

  • Calendar
    • Previous data
    • Upcoming events
  • “Projects” lists
    • Evaluate the status of projects, goals, and outcomes one by one
  • “Next Actions” lists
    • Mark off completed actions
    • Review reminders
  • “Waiting For” list
    • Record appropriate actions for any needed follow-up
    • Check off received items
  • Checklists
  • “Someday/Maybe” list
    • Check for any projects that may have been active
    • Transfer them to “Projects” list
    • Delete items no longer of interest
  • “Pending” and Support Files
